Every culture has its own sound.

Music is one of humanity's oldest forms of expression. Across continents, communities have created instruments from the materials available around them.

From the delicate strings of the Japanese koto to the powerful rhythms of the West African djembe, instruments carry stories of people, places and traditions.

FEATURED INSTRUMENT

The Sitar

The sitar is one of the best-known instruments associated with Hindustani classical music. Its distinctive sound comes from a combination of playing strings and sympathetic strings.

THE SCIENCE OF SOUND

How Instruments Create Music

01

Strings

Vibrating strings produce sound. Their pitch changes according to their length, tension and thickness.

02

Air

Wind instruments create sound by making a column of air vibrate inside the instrument.

03

Percussion

Drums create sound when surfaces are struck, shaken, scraped or otherwise set into motion.

04

Electronics

Electronic instruments generate and manipulate sound using electrical and digital technology.
